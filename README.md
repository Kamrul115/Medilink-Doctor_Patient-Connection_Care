# MediLink

MediLink is an innovative healthcare service platform designed to bridge the gap between patients, doctors, and healthcare delivery personnel through a unified digital ecosystem. Developed as an academic and practical solution, the platform simplifies appointment scheduling, digital record management, and medicine delivery.

## Core Features
* **Appointment Scheduling:** Patients can easily search for specialists, check doctor availability, and book consultations.
* **Digital Prescription & Report Management:** Doctors can manage appointments, generate consultations, and issue secure digital prescriptions and medical reports.
* **Medicine Ordering & Delivery:** Direct medicine ordering through the platform paired with real-time delivery tracking via assigned riders.
* **Role-Based Access:** Dedicated interfaces for patients, doctors, administrators, and delivery riders.
